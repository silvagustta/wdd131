Site Name

Motocycle.inc

Motocycle.inc is a website focused on motorcycles and motorcycle enthusiasts. The name combines the word "Motocycle" with "inc.", giving the project a modern and professional identity related to the motorcycle industry.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Motocycle.inc is to provide a simple and organized place where motorcycle enthusiasts and people interested in motorcycles can find information about different motorcycle models and categories.

  • Motorcycle models and specifications.
  • Information about different motorcycle categories.
  • Images of motorcycles.
  • Basic information about engines and performance.
  • Comparisons between different motorcycles.

Scenarios

The website is designed for people who are interested in motorcycles and want to learn more about different models.

  • What are the main specifications of this motorcycle?
  • Which motorcycle is good for someone who is starting to ride?
  • What are the different categories of motorcycles?
  • Which motorcycles have better performance?
  • Where can I find information about a specific motorcycle?

Text color

The dark color will be used for the header, navigation, main sections, and footer. The red color will be used for headings, buttons, links, and important elements. The light color will be used for text on dark backgrounds.

Typography

Oswald

Oswald will be used for main headings, page titles, navigation, motorcycle names, and section headings.

Arial

Arial will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, motorcycle specifications, and general body text.
